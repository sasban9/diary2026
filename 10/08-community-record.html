Kolkata Community Record

Every CommunityDeserves a Record.

We are building a digital and physical record of Kolkata's under-documented communities — one household, one person and one year at a time.

Join the pilotLearn how it works

Why

Millions of people. Very little record.

Millions of people live and work in communities that have very little organized documentation of their history, skills, families, achievements and access to government services.

We want to change that.

Identity

What if your community had a permanent identity?

Every participating community receives its own Community Record. A unique community ID connects:

  • The community
  • Households
  • Residents
  • Annual photographs
  • Community history
  • Skills and occupations
  • Government-benefit access
  • Community events
  • Applications and assistance
  • Participation over time

Instead of simply saying:

"We live here."

there is a structured record showing:

"This community was documented here in 2026, and its history continues year after year."

Every Year

Your Community. Your Record. Every Year.

Every year, we create a dated community photograph and update the community record.

2026📸 Community photograph100 households287 residents documented
2027📸 Community photograph104 households301 residents documented
2028📸 Community photograph109 households316 residents documented

Over time, the community develops its own visual and documentary history.

2026→2027→2028→2029→2030

A simple message emerges:

"We were here."

Note: The photograph documents participation and presence on a particular date; it is not, by itself, legal proof of residence.

Households

Every Household Gets a Record

A household can have a structured profile containing information such as:

  • Household members
  • Occupations
  • Education
  • Skills
  • Children
  • Senior citizens
  • Government benefits received
  • Potentially relevant benefits
  • Year first documented
  • Participation history

Private by design. Sensitive documents remain private. Public pages should never expose Aadhaar numbers, bank details, or other sensitive personal information.

Benefits Navigation

We Don't Just Document People. We Help Them Access What Already Exists.

Many families may be eligible for government schemes and services but may not know:

  • What they are eligible for
  • Where to apply
  • What documents they need
  • How to complete the process
  • Whether their application has progressed

Our model is:

DocumentIdentifyVerifyAssistTrack

For example:

The objective is simple:

Help families access benefits they are already entitled to.

Participation

Your Contribution Can Become a Record of Participation

Membership continuity can be recorded factually. For example:

Member since2026
Payments47 / 48 months
Current statusActive

Over several years, someone could build a record showing consistent participation — a longitudinal socioeconomic participation record.

Not a credit score. This does not claim that someone is creditworthy, and should not be represented as something banks or government agencies automatically recognize. It simply records participation and continuity.

Representatives

Communities Need Representatives

Every community can have a local representative. Representatives help us:

  • Understand the community
  • Identify families
  • Maintain records
  • Organize annual documentation
  • Identify common problems
  • Help residents navigate services
  • Keep the information updated

Representatives are not political agents. They are community coordinators.
