# Course: AI-Powered Digital Safety – Protect Your Identity, Privacy & Digital Footprint



Practical, project-based course

Beginner friendly

2026-ready curriculum



The central idea should be to teach people how to use AI as their personal digital security assistant — helping them identify threats, protect their identities, reduce their digital footprints, detect scams and maintain long-term online privacy.

This is more than a cybersecurity course. Traditional cybersecurity teaches people how to protect their devices and networks. This course should teach ordinary people how to protect their entire digital lives.

## 1. Course positioning

Suggested title:

AI & Your Digital Safety: Become Invisible to Scammers, Hackers and Data Exploiters

Alternative titles:

- Your Digital Bodyguard: Using AI to Stay Safe Online
- Digital Footprint Detox: Reclaim Your Online Privacy with AI
- AI-Powered Cyber Safety for Everyone
- The Privacy-First Internet: Protect Your Identity in the AI Era

Core promise:

By the end of this course, students will know how to use AI tools to audit their digital exposure, secure their accounts, identify malicious communication, protect personal information and build safer online habits.

Target audience

- Students and teenagers
- Parents protecting their families
- Working professionals
- Freelancers and entrepreneurs
- Senior citizens
- Small business owners
- Anyone using social media, online banking, email and digital payments

I would avoid long theoretical lectures. Every lesson should follow:

1. Explain a real-world risk.
2. Demonstrate how the risk works.
3. Show how AI helps identify or mitigate it.
4. Give students a task to complete on their own devices.

## 7. A particularly interesting positioning opportunity

Rather than marketing this as another cybersecurity course, position it around a bigger emerging problem:

[Digital Footprint: Who Really Knows You?](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Zg0bMue2UrVj_qvcv27c906CnefN5aKaHqiOaZYjNsR9WrYdqSiBmrznIcLjxZA8P8wmdxYiD-IJQs1f1ukJRV-LvLboWGZaDTpjitaxMT0-m_8vkqGVe2H6bWKDO36Rx4ZLM7_W8_hoLtR_nGndidAmpuuw-I8crYvAqpfe5v0?purpose=inline)

[alcyone.si](https://www.alcyone.si/en/blog/post/view/id/digital-footprint-who-really-knows-you?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Your digital identity is becoming more valuable than your physical identity.

Your photographs, voice, browsing history, financial activity, location, professional history and social connections collectively create a digital representation of you.

AI makes it easier to:

- Impersonate you.
- Predict your behaviour.
- Profile your preferences.
- Manipulate your decisions.
- Exploit your relationships.
- Generate convincing fake evidence.

People need to learn how to defend themselves in this environment.

This is an excellent opportunity to teach digital self-defence as a basic life skill, much like financial literacy.
