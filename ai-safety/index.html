Last updated: Nov 6, 2026

Summary: Sasban's course "Safety in AI World" — full 8-module outline (36 lessons, 63 videos), lesson hook questions, 5-min video rule

Details: Building a course called "Safety in AI World" (same as the project name) Videos are maximum 5 minutes each; any lesson longer than that gets broken into multiple parts Wants modules to be deeper with differing numbers of lessons per module Every lesson should open with one mind-blowing hook question to grab the learner
Course outline (saved at user's request; 8 modules, 36 lessons, 63 videos, ~5 hrs) course structure — each lesson has a real-world example and hands-on activity; each module ends with a one-page checklist; videos max 5 min, long lessons split into parts a/b/c; each lesson opens with one hook question (in part a, first ~20s) answered by the end of the lesson

Module 1: How AI Actually Works (4 lessons, 6 videos)

  • 1.1 What AI Is (a chatbots/assistants, b image/video/voice AI) — hook: Is the thing you're chatting with thinking, or just guessing the next word?
  • 1.2 How AI Produces Answers (a pattern prediction not understanding, b why confident but wrong) — hook: How can a machine write a perfect essay and still have no idea what's true?
  • 1.3 What AI Can and Can't Do — hook: What does a toddler do effortlessly that the smartest AI still fumbles?
  • 1.4 AI Around You Every Day — hook: How many decisions about you did AI make before you finished breakfast?

Module 2: Protecting Your Privacy (3 lessons, 7 videos)

  • 2.1 Where Your Data Goes (a prompts/uploads, b voice recordings and retention) — hook: When you hit send, who else gets to read what you just typed?
  • 2.2 Controlling Your Settings (a training opt-outs/chat history, b memory/incognito, c walkthrough across popular apps) — hook: What if one hidden toggle decides whether your private chats train the next AI?
  • 2.3 The Never-Share List (a what never to paste, b redact-before-you-paste habit) — hook: What's one sentence you've typed into an AI that you'd never say out loud in a crowded room?
  • Module 2 activity — audit privacy settings on your two most-used AI tools

Module 3: Scams, Deepfakes, and Impersonation (5 lessons, 9 videos)

  • 3.1 How AI Supercharges Scams (1 video) — hook: What if the scam message in your inbox were written better than your own emails?
  • 3.2 Voice Cloning and the Emergency Call Scam (a few seconds of audio is enough, b real cases) — hook: If your mother called in tears begging for money, how would you know it's really her?
  • 3.3 Deepfake Video and Images (a visual tells and why they fade, b verification beats detection) — hook: Can you trust your own eyes when a video of you saying something you never said takes minutes to make?
  • 3.4 Verification Habits (a family safe words and call-back rules, b pause-and-check routine) — hook: What single word, agreed over dinner, could stop a ₹10 lakh scam?
  • 3.5 If You've Been Targeted (a reporting and freezing accounts, b recovery steps) — hook: You just sent money to a scammer. What you do in the next hour matters most, so what is it?
  • Module 3 activity — spot the fake among mixed real/AI samples, agree a family safe word

Module 4: Trusting (and Checking) AI Output (4 lessons, 7 videos)

  • 4.1 Why AI Gets Things Wrong (a hallucinations/invented citations, b outdated knowledge) — hook: What if AI cited a book so convincingly that the book doesn't exist?
  • 4.2 Bias and Blind Spots (1 video) — hook: Whose voices are missing from everything AI learned from?
  • 4.3 The Two-Minute Fact-Check (a cross-checking sources, b asking AI to show reasoning) — hook: Can you catch an AI's mistake in under two minutes?
  • 4.4 High-Stakes Zones (a health, b legal and money) — hook: When does a confident wrong answer stop being embarrassing and start being dangerous?
  • Module 4 activity — catch the errors in a deliberately flawed AI answer

Module 5: Emotional Wellbeing and Healthy Use (6 lessons, 10 videos)

5.1 Why AI Feels Like a Person (1 video) — hook: Why does it feel rude to say goodbye to a machine? 5.2 Flattery and Agreement (a why AI tells you what you want to hear, b how to ask for honest pushback) — hook: Is AI agreeing with you because you're right, or because you're you? 5.3 Companion Apps and Attachment (a benefits and risks, b business models) — hook: What happens when someone's best friend is a product with a subscription fee? 5.4 Signs of Over-Reliance (1 video) — hook: When did you last go a full day without asking AI anything? 5.5 Using AI During Hard Times (a what it can and can't do, b when to reach a human or crisis service) — hook: At 2 a.m., when you're hurting, who should you reach for first? 5.6 Building a Balanced Routine (a usage boundaries, b keeping human relationships first) — hook: What would a perfect day with AI look like, and where would it stay out of the way? Module 5 activity — one-week usage journal and reflection

Module 6: Safe Use at School and Work (5 lessons, 8 videos)

6.1 Academic Integrity and Disclosure (a help vs cheating line, b honest disclosure) — hook: If AI wrote your essay but you did the thinking, whose work is it? 6.2 Confidential Data at Work (1 video) — hook: What if one pasted spreadsheet leaked your company's secrets? 6.3 Copyright and Ownership (a what you can feed into AI, b who owns/can publish output) — hook: When AI creates something, who owns it? 6.4 Company AI Policies (1 video) — hook: Does your workplace already have AI rules you've never read? 6.5 Building Skills, Not Outsourcing Thinking (a do it yourself first, b AI as coach not crutch) — hook: If AI does your thinking for a year, what happens to your own mind?

Module 7: Kids, Teens, and Families (6 lessons, 9 videos)

7.1 Age-Appropriate Use (1 video) — hook: At what age should a child first talk to a machine that talks back? 7.2 Parental Controls and Settings (a account/app controls, b device-level and school tools) — hook: Can one setting stand between your child and something you'd never allow in your home? 7.3 Talking to Young People About AI (a younger kids, b teens) — hook: What if your child knows more about AI than you do, yet believes everything it says? 7.4 AI-Generated Bullying and Image Abuse (a what it looks like and how to respond, b reporting and support) — hook: What if a classmate could fake a photo of your child in 30 seconds? 7.5 Online Manipulation and Red Flags (1 video) — hook: How do you spot a bot trying to change what a teenager believes? 7.6 Your Family AI Agreement (1 video) — hook: What if every member of your family, parents included, signed one page of AI rules?

Module 8: The Bigger Picture (3 lessons, 7 videos)

8.1 Safety, Regulation, and Accountability (a who builds/tests/governs AI, b your rights and where to report) — hook: When AI harms someone, who's responsible: the maker, the user, or no one? 8.2 Jobs and the Future of Work (a what's changing and what isn't, b skills that stay valuable) — hook: Which of your skills would still matter if AI did your job's tasks tomorrow? 8.3 Your Personal AI Safety Plan, capstone (a audit your habits, b write your plan, c set reminders and share it) — hook: If you could write one rule to protect yourself from AI for the next ten years, what would it be?
